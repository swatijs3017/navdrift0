AdaptiMount™ NOVEL

PhaseNet™ Vibration Filter NOVEL
Adaptive notch filter that identifies engine RPM harmonics in the IMU spectrum and removes them in real-time. Preserves navigation-relevant accelerations while eliminating chassis noise.
