Core Principles

Algorithm × Precision

01

Quaternion SO(3)
Pre-integration

Heading tracked on the SO(3) Lie group via quaternion multiplication. No gimbal lock. No Euler-angle singularities. Pure continuous attitude throughout manoeuvres.

02

Adaptive RL
Noise Tuning

PPO-style online reinforcement loop adapts the EKF Q/R matrices in real time. The filter tightens in steady motion and relaxes through hard manoeuvres — automatically.

03

Sensor Fault
Detection

Per-channel z-score anomaly scoring flags gyro, accelerometer, and GPS glitches the instant they deviate. Corrupted measurements are isolated before they reach the filter.

04

Uncertainty-Aware
Routing

EKF covariance ellipses propagate live onto the map. Route planning weights paths by positioning confidence — safer choices when uncertainty grows.

05

Tunnel Dead-Reckoning
Predictor

Barometric slope analysis predicts GNSS reacquisition ETA before the tunnel ends. The system prepares for handoff — seamless position continuity at the exit.

06

Federated
Fleet Learning

Gradient aggregation across 12 simulated vehicles. Every road encounter — pothole, sharp corner, bridge — improves the shared model without sharing raw data.

The Navigation
Pipeline

From raw sensor noise to a trusted position fix — four stages of intelligent signal processing, running continuously at 200 Hz.

Stage 01
IMU Preprocessing

Per-axis z-score fault detection. Bias estimation and temperature compensation on raw gyro + accelerometer streams.

Stage 02
DRIFTFormer AI

12-head Transformer attention over 64-step IMU history. Predicts velocity and heading — even inside tunnels with zero satellite signal.

Stage 03
NavIC + GNSS Fusion

Quaternion EKF on SO(3) fuses GPS, NavIC, and DRIFTFormer outputs. Adaptive RL continuously tunes process and measurement noise.

Stage 04
Position Output

Uncertainty-weighted position fix with live covariance ellipse. Route planner selects paths by confidence. Fleet gradient sync every 30 s.

Built for ISRO SIH 2026 PS #26168. NAVDRIFT-0 targets sub-metre lateral error in worst-case urban canyon and 60-second tunnel scenarios — the benchmark conditions specified in the problem statement. All processing runs on embedded hardware at full frame rate.
